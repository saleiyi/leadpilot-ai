## Buyer questions

1. What does your business sell?
2. Where do inquiries arrive today?
3. What makes a lead high priority?
4. Which software should receive the result?
5. Should the system only draft replies, or can it send any low-risk replies automatically?
6. Please provide your real services, policies, tone, and escalation rules.
7. Do you have authorized administrator/API access to every requested integration?

## Scope boundary

The default delivery drafts responses for human review. Automatic sending, regulated advice, payment actions, refunds, account deletion, and binding commercial commitments require a separately approved scope and additional safeguards.
