# Client delivery checklist

- [ ] Confirm the client's legal business name and authorized contact.
- [ ] Confirm the exact input channel and destination system.
- [ ] Receive written services, prices, policies, and escalation rules.
- [ ] Use client-owned API credentials; never ask for passwords in chat.
- [ ] Keep secrets in server-side environment variables.
- [ ] Test at least 10 normal, 5 ambiguous, and 5 adversarial inquiries.
- [ ] Verify the Agent never invents prices, deadlines, refunds, or guarantees.
- [ ] Verify sensitive and uncertain cases route to a human.
- [ ] Record a handover video without exposing secrets or customer data.
- [ ] Give the client a data-retention and deletion procedure.
- [ ] Get written approval before enabling any automatic send action.
